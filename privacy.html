Privacy policy

This covers Story Studio, my personal tool that runs on my own computer. Last updated 31 October 2026.

The short version

Story Studio has no servers and no other users. It collects nothing about anyone, shares nothing with anyone, and keeps everything on my own computer. It only connects to accounts I own.

What it can access on each account

YouTube (Google). Permission to upload videos to my channel, to read basic information about my channel so it can show which channel is connected, and to manage the videos it uploaded, so it can add a thumbnail, a subtitle file and a comment to them.

TikTok. Permission to read my basic profile (my display name and account id) so it can show which account is connected, and to send videos to my TikTok drafts. Posting from the drafts is something I do myself in the TikTok app.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta). Permission to see the Facebook Pages I manage and the Instagram account linked to my Page, and to publish Reels to them.

How that access is used

Only to do what I ask for: posting a video I made, with the title, description, thumbnail and schedule I reviewed before pressing the button. Nothing is posted without that confirmation. On YouTube it also adds a comment on my own Shorts linking to the full version of the same story.

How it is stored

The access tokens each platform issues are stored in the macOS Keychain on my computer, which the operating system encrypts. They are never written to a file, never included in logs, and never leave my computer except in requests sent directly to that platform's own servers over HTTPS.

Story Studio also keeps a local record of what it posted and when, so it never posts the same video twice. That record holds video titles and ids, and no passwords or tokens.

Sharing

None. No data from any platform is passed to any other person, company, server or service. There is no analytics, no tracking, no error reporting and no advertising.

Google API Services

Story Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting

I can disconnect any account inside Story Studio at any time. That deletes its tokens from the Keychain and asks the platform to revoke them. Access can also be removed from each platform's own settings: the Google account permissions page, the app permissions section of TikTok's settings, and the apps and business integrations sections of Instagram's and Facebook's settings.

Children

Story Studio is not directed at children. It is used by one adult, me.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
