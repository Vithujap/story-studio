Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Story Studio is a desktop app for publishing videos to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook from one place. I made it to publish my own videos: it runs on my own computer and connects only to accounts I own. This policy covers every platform Story Studio connects to. It explains what information the app uses, why, where it is kept, for how long, and how to remove it. Connecting an account in Story Studio means agreeing to this policy.

In short

  • Story Studio has no servers, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics.
  • It connects to a platform only after you sign in and approve access on that platform's own page.
  • It uses a platform's information only to publish the videos you choose and to show which account is connected.
  • It does not sell or share any information.
  • You can remove its access at any time, and stored information is deleted on request within 7 days.

Information it uses

From each connected platform:

  • Account details: the account's name, username, profile picture, id and follower count, to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: issued by the platform when you sign in, so Story Studio can publish to that account.
  • Posting options: what the platform allows the account to do, such as who can see a post, so the right choices are offered.
  • Post results: the ids, links and status the platform returns for each post, to show whether it went through.

From you: the videos, titles, captions, thumbnails, subtitles and settings you prepare for each post.

What it does on each platform

YouTube (Google). Permissions: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, and youtube.force-ssl, which is requested only when subtitle upload or the comment is on, and used only to add subtitle tracks and that comment. Story Studio uploads videos with the title, description, thumbnail and subtitles you set, publishes them now or at the time you choose, adds the comment you approved on the review sheet under your own Shorts, linking to the full video, reads the status of the videos it uploaded, and reads the channel's name, handle, id and subscriber count.

TikTok. Permissions: user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish, which is requested only when Story Studio posts for you. It reads your display name, profile picture and account id. Before a post, it reads your username and the options TikTok allows your account: who can see a post, whether comments, duets and stitches can be turned on, and the longest video accepted. It sends a video to your TikTok inbox to finish in the TikTok app, or posts it with the caption, visibility, interaction and commercial content settings you choose, and reads whether each post went through.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta). Permissions: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish. Story Studio reads the Facebook Pages you manage and the Instagram account linked to your Page (their names, ids, follower and post counts, and how many posts Instagram still allows today), publishes Reels to them, and reads whether each Reel went through.

How it is used

Only to do what you ask for in Story Studio: publish a video you selected and reviewed, with the title, caption, thumbnail, visibility and time you chose, show which account is connected, and show whether each post went through. Nothing is posted until you press Send. No information is used for advertising or profiling, and none is sold.

Where it is kept, and how it is protected

  • Access tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain, which the operating system encrypts. They are never written to a file or a log, and are sent only to the platform that issued them, over HTTPS.
  • Account details, the post record and its log are kept in files on my computer that only my user account can read.
  • Nothing is kept anywhere else. Story Studio has no servers.

How long it is kept

  • Access tokens: until you sign out, or the platform ends them.
  • Account details: refreshed from the platform each day Story Studio runs while the account is connected, and deleted when you sign out. Details that cannot be refreshed for 30 days are deleted.
  • Post results: the ids and links a platform returned are deleted once they are 30 days old, or when you sign out of that platform, whichever comes first. Signing out also cancels a comment still waiting to be posted.
  • Post record: so the same video is never posted twice, Story Studio keeps a fingerprint of each video file it sent, with the platform, the time, the result, and the title, caption and comment you wrote. This stays until you delete it.
  • Channel lock: if you choose to only ever upload to one YouTube channel, that channel's id is kept in Story Studio's settings, so it never uploads anywhere else.

Sharing

None. No information from any platform is sold, shared or passed to any other person, company, server or service. Story Studio shows no advertising, lets no third party serve content in it, and has no analytics and no error reporting.

Cookies and device storage

This website sets no cookies and runs no tracking. Story Studio stores the information described above on the computer it runs on, and nowhere else.

Removing access and deleting data

In Story Studio: on the Accounts page, press Sign out. This deletes the account's access tokens, asks the platform to revoke them, and deletes the account's details and the ids and links that platform returned.

On each platform:

  • Google and YouTube: in addition to signing out in Story Studio, you can revoke its access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, then remove Story Studio.
  • Instagram and Facebook: the apps and websites, or business integrations, section of each app's settings, then remove Story Studio.

By email: to have any information Story Studio holds about you deleted, email the address below. It is deleted as soon as possible and within 7 days, and the deletion is confirmed by email.

Deleting information in Story Studio does not delete anything on the platforms. Videos you posted stay there until you remove them on that platform.

Security
