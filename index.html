Story Studio

My personal tool for making short story videos and posting them to my own accounts.

Story Studio runs on my own computer. I use it to put together short videos that tell stories, with captions and background footage, and to post those videos to my own channels.

What it does with my accounts

  • YouTube: uploads my videos with the title, description, thumbnail and subtitles I reviewed, and adds a comment on my own Shorts linking to the full version of the story.
  • TikTok: sends my videos to my TikTok drafts, so I can review and post them from the TikTok app.
  • Instagram and Facebook: posts my videos as Reels on my own Instagram account and Facebook Page.

Who uses it

Only me. It is not a service, it has no other users, there is nothing to sign up for, and it is not hosted online. It only ever posts to accounts I own.
