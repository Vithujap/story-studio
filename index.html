Story Studio

One place to publish my videos to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Story Studio is the desktop app I use to publish my own short story videos. I prepare a video once, check its title, caption and thumbnail, and send it to each platform from one screen, straight away or at a time I choose.

What it does on each platform

  • YouTube: uploads my videos with the title, description, thumbnail and subtitles I set, publishes them now or at the time I choose, and adds a comment on my own Shorts linking to the full video. It shows the name of the connected channel. Story Studio uses YouTube API Services.
  • TikTok: shows which account is connected, by its display name, username and profile picture. It either sends a video to my TikTok inbox, where I edit and post it in the TikTok app, or posts it with the caption and the settings I choose before pressing Send: who can see it, whether others can comment, duet or stitch, and whether it is commercial content.
  • Instagram and Facebook: publishes my videos as Reels on my Instagram account and my Facebook Page.

Before anything is posted

Every post appears in a review step, showing the account it goes to, its visibility and time, and everything that will be set. I can change every title, description, caption and tag there. A comment under a Short is posted only if I leave it ticked. Nothing is sent until I press Send, and I can cancel a scheduled post at any time before it goes out.

Where it runs

Story Studio runs on my own computer and connects only to accounts I own. It has no servers and no sign-up.
